## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Generate SVG mock-ups from Ewoks workflows with `graph_to_svg`. Tasks are
  arranged horizontally and show their names, inputs, and outputs in readable,
  automatically sized boxes.
- Draw workflow links as smooth curves with customizable colors, widths, and dash
  patterns.
- Convert Ewoks workflows into graphs suitable for automatic layered layout,
  including task dimensions and connections.
- Add Pixi as an installation option.
